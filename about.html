About Notes Hunt

Hi, I’m Mani Sai Charan Reddy, the creator of Notes Hunt.

Notes Hunt was created with a simple goal — to help students get easy access to quality study notes and prepare well for their exams.

While I was studying, I often faced a common problem. I had to ask my friends again and again: “Send me that PDF”, “Do you have those notes?”, “Can you share the material?”. Important notes were scattered across WhatsApp chats, emails, and drives, making exam preparation stressful and time-consuming.

To solve this problem, I decided to build Notes Hunt — a single platform where students can find organized, reliable, and easy-to-access notes without depending on others.

This website is made by a student, for students. My aim is to support learners by saving their time, reducing stress, and helping them focus on what truly matters — understanding concepts and performing well in exams.

Notes Hunt will continue to grow with more subjects, better organization, and useful resources to help students at every stage of their academic journey.
